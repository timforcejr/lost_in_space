- **What 8-bit weights do to an explanation.** The same browser numbers against PyTorch with everything in full precision. The score moves by about 0.01, but for near-identical pairs the explanation moves much more: the gradient of a cosine close to 1 is tiny, and its direction is set by the small difference between the two embeddings, which 8-bit rounding changes.
